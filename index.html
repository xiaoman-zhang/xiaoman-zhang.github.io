Xiaoman Zhang 张小嫚

Hi, I am a tenure-track Associate Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Biomedical Informatics at Harvard University, working with Prof. Pranav Rajpurkar. I completed my Ph.D. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and my B.S. at the School for the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China.

My research focuses on developing AI systems that advance our understanding of biological mechanisms and accelerate therapeutic discovery through predictive modeling, scientific reasoning, and experimental learning.

Research

We aim to build AI systems that bridge biological prediction and mechanistic discovery, enabling a deeper understanding of living systems and accelerating the discovery of new therapeutics.

Full Publication List

Understand

Knowledge-Guided Scientific Reasoning

Integrate biological knowledge and evidence to reason about molecular relationships and mechanisms.

SPIDER: Evidence-Grounded Biomedical Knowledge and Reasoning

SPIDER is a multi-agent framework that grounds biomedical knowledge graph relations in literature evidence and study conditions, helping distinguish contextual differences from genuine scientific disagreement.
The resulting SPIDER-KG connects biological relationships to their supporting evidence and experimental context, with daily updates from newly indexed PubMed records.
SPIDER improves the interpretation of conflicting findings, while SPIDER-KG supports evidence-grounded biomedical reasoning and helps contextualize emerging discoveries.

Predict

Biological Foundation Models

Model biological systems and predict their responses to perturbations.

MAP: A Knowledge-driven Framework for Predicting Single-cell Responses for Unprofiled Drugs
Jinghao Feng*, Ziheng Zhao*, Xiaoman Zhang*, Mingfei Liu, Jingyi Chen, Xingran Quan, Boyang Fu, Jian Zhang, Yanfeng Wang, Ya Zhang, Weidi Xie
Nature Machine Intelligence (5-Year Impact Factor: 36.0)
Most existing models for predicting drug-induced single-cell responses fail to generalize beyond their training compound set, limiting in-silico screening to a narrow chemical space. MAP introduces a knowledge-driven framework that grounds predictions in molecular structure and biological pathway priors, enabling transcriptomic response prediction for drugs never seen during training. This opens the door to systematic in-silico screening across novel chemical libraries — a key step toward predictive, simulation-driven drug discovery.
Phenotypic Bioactivity Prediction as Open-set Biological Assay Querying
Yuze Sun*, Xiaoman Zhang*, Qiaoyu Zheng, Hanzheng Li, Jianming Zhang, Liang Hong, Yanfeng Wang, Ya Zhang, Weidi Xie
Nature Chemical Biology (5-Year Impact Factor: 16.2), in press
Phenotypic compound profiling is fundamentally open-ended: each new biological assay defines a new target space that models trained on fixed assay panels cannot handle. We reformulate phenotypic bioactivity prediction as an open-set retrieval problem in which assays themselves are treated as queryable entities. The resulting framework generalizes to unseen targets and assays, enabling scalable, hypothesis-free compound profiling at the scale needed for real-world screening campaigns.

Research Internships

We are looking for research interns interested in AI for biological discovery. Outstanding interns may be considered for PhD opportunities starting in 2028.

Research Direction

AI for Biological Discovery. Develop AI systems for agentic scientific reasoning, biological foundation models, and scientific discovery through experimental feedback, with the goal of understanding biological mechanisms and accelerating therapeutic discovery.

Who We Are Looking For

  • Curiosity about AI and life sciences, with strong motivation and independent thinking.
  • A solid foundation in mathematics and computing, familiarity with machine learning, and good programming skills.
  • Students from CS, EE, biomedical engineering, biology, and related fields are welcome.

What You Will Get

  • Hands-on mentorship, from problem formulation and method design to experiments and scientific writing.
  • Research and computing resources at SJTU's School of Artificial Intelligence.
  • Opportunities to collaborate across AI and the life sciences.

How to Apply

Email xiaomanzhang.zxm@gmail.com with the subject “Research Internship – Name – University – Year”. Include your CV, a brief introduction to your research interests and relevant experience, links to representative projects or papers, and your expected availability.

研究方向

围绕大语言模型与多智能体协作，研究面向天体物理科学问题的智能体方法，探索 AI 如何整合科学知识、使用专业工具，并完成复杂的科学研究任务。目前已有明确的研究课题与实施计划，期待同学深度参与方法设计、系统开发与实验验证，在真实科学问题中探索智能体的能力与边界。

你将获得

  • 交叉学科指导：与人工智能研究者和天文学家合作，了解真实科研需求，开展有科学价值的方法研究。
  • 完整科研训练：参与从问题定义、算法设计到实验分析和论文写作的完整过程，逐步培养独立科研能力。
  • 深造机会：课题组优先从科研实习生中招收博士和硕士研究生，也会为有意申请海外高校的同学提供推荐信。

我们希望你

  • 对 AI for Science、多智能体以及天体物理交叉研究有热情，具有较强的自我驱动能力。
  • 具备扎实的数理与计算机基础，了解基本的机器学习、深度学习算法，具有良好的编程能力。
  • 每周能够投入 15–20 小时开展科研，愿意持续推进课题、主动沟通并与团队合作。
  • 有大语言模型、智能体开发或天文学相关经历者优先；不要求已有天文学背景，但希望你愿意主动学习跨学科知识。
